  1. 195001 / 15
    FoundationsOctober 1950

    Alan Turing Proposes the Imitation Game

    Alan Turing publishes Computing Machinery and Intelligence, introducing the imitation game as a way to explore whether machines can demonstrate intelligent behavior.

    PEOPLE
    • Alan Turing
  2. 195602 / 15
    Foundations1956

    Artificial Intelligence Becomes a Research Field

    The Dartmouth Summer Research Project brings researchers together to explore machine intelligence and helps establish artificial intelligence as a formal field of study.

    PEOPLE
    • John McCarthy
    • Marvin Minsky
    • Claude Shannon
    • Nathaniel Rochester
  3. 195803 / 15
    Research1958

    The Perceptron

    Frank Rosenblatt demonstrates the perceptron, an early neural network model designed to learn patterns from examples.

    PEOPLE
    • Frank Rosenblatt
  4. 196604 / 15
    Applications1966

    ELIZA Demonstrates Early Conversation

    Joseph Weizenbaum creates ELIZA, a computer program that simulates conversation using pattern matching and scripted responses.

    PEOPLE
    • Joseph Weizenbaum
  5. 197205 / 15
    Applications1972

    MYCIN and Expert Systems

    Researchers at Stanford begin developing MYCIN, a rule-based system designed to assist with identifying bacterial infections and recommending antibiotic treatments.

    PEOPLE
    • Edward Shortliffe
  6. 198606 / 15
    Research1986

    Backpropagation Advances Neural Networks

    David Rumelhart, Geoffrey Hinton, and Ronald Williams publish influential research demonstrating how backpropagation can train multilayer neural networks.

    PEOPLE
    • David Rumelhart
    • Geoffrey Hinton
    • Ronald Williams
  7. 199707 / 15
    ApplicationsMay 1997

    Deep Blue Defeats Garry Kasparov

    IBM's Deep Blue defeats world chess champion Garry Kasparov in a six-game match, demonstrating the power of specialized computer systems in strategic decision-making.

    PEOPLE
    • Garry Kasparov
  8. 200908 / 15
    Research2009

    ImageNet Accelerates Computer Vision

    ImageNet provides a large labeled image dataset that becomes a major resource for training and evaluating computer vision systems.

    PEOPLE
    • Fei-Fei Li
    • Jia Deng
  9. 201209 / 15
    Research2012

    AlexNet Transforms Image Recognition

    AlexNet achieves a major improvement in the ImageNet competition, demonstrating the effectiveness of deep convolutional neural networks trained using GPUs.

    PEOPLE
    • Alex Krizhevsky
    • Ilya Sutskever
    • Geoffrey Hinton
  10. 201610 / 15
    ApplicationsMarch 2016

    AlphaGo Defeats Lee Sedol

    Google DeepMind's AlphaGo defeats professional Go player Lee Sedol in a five-game match, demonstrating advances in deep learning and reinforcement learning.

    PEOPLE
    • Lee Sedol
  11. 201711 / 15
    Research2017

    The Transformer Architecture

    Researchers publish Attention Is All You Need, introducing the Transformer architecture that becomes foundational to many modern large language models.

    PEOPLE
    • Ashish Vaswani
    • Noam Shazeer
    • Niki Parmar
  12. 202012 / 15
    Generative AIMay 2020

    GPT-3 Demonstrates Large-Scale Language Generation

    OpenAI introduces GPT-3, a large language model that demonstrates strong few-shot learning and text-generation capabilities.

  13. 202213 / 15
    Generative AI30 November 2022

    ChatGPT Launches Publicly

    OpenAI releases ChatGPT as a research preview, making conversational generative AI widely accessible and prompting new debates about education, work, and trust.

  14. 202314 / 15
    Generative AI14 March 2023

    GPT-4 Advances Multimodal AI

    OpenAI introduces GPT-4, a model capable of processing text and image inputs, demonstrating improved performance across many complex tasks.

  15. 202415 / 15
    Governance1 August 2024

    The EU AI Act Enters Into Force

    The European Union's AI Act enters into force, establishing a risk-based regulatory framework for artificial intelligence, with requirements applying in phases.
